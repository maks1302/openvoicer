# Code of conduct

OpenVoicer is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about technical concerns.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect private information, including media, recordings, paths, and project data shared during debugging.
- Credit the work and ideas of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory conduct
- Sexualized language or attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing copyrighted or private media obtained through the project without authorization
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the OpenVoicer community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Until a dedicated conduct address is published, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Enforcement decisions should prioritize community safety, proportionality, and a clear explanation to the affected parties where appropriate.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by widely used open-source community codes of conduct and is intentionally written in concise project-specific language.
